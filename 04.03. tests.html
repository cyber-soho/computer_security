How to study from this hub

1 · Flash-cards first

Twenty-five cards, five per lesson: read the question, say the answer aloud, flip, mark "I know this". Repeat until every card is known.

2 · Then the practice test

Twenty-five new scenario questions (15 with one right answer, 10 with several — the number expected is shown in brackets). Use the hints before you reveal.

3 · Then your Binder

Redo the Lab 4.2 priority table with new numbers you invent; reread the scope statement; rewrite the executive summary from memory. The Mid-Term uses the same shapes.
