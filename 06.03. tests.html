How the quiz works

  • One question at a time. Press Show hint if you are stuck, then Check my answer to see an explanation for every option.
  • Every MSQ (Multiple-Select Question — several correct answers) shows in round brackets how many answers are expected, for example (select 3), and counts as correct only when exactly those answers are selected.
  • The results show your score by lesson and by question type, with a review list. You can retry only the missed questions, or try again with the questions in a new order.
  • Keyboard: in the flash cards, the arrow keys move and the space bar flips the card; in the quiz, the keys 1 to 6 select an option.

Your known cards and your best score are kept in this browser only.
